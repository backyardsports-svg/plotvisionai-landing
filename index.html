Who is behind it

Built by someone who has installed the work, not just rendered it.

PlotVisionAI comes out of Landforms Design Inc, the landscape design and build company Darin Chamberlin founded in February 1995. Darin is the owner and lead designer, with more than 42 years in horticulture and landscape construction — the majority of it spent on the same properties he was drawing, which is a different education than drawing alone.

That background is the reason this tool behaves the way it does. A concept has to survive the parts of the job nobody photographs: how water leaves the site, whether a wall wants a step or a curve, what a fixture actually does to a maple at ten at night, how much room a court really needs once you add the fence and the run-off. Renderings that ignore those things create arguments later. The point here is to put the decision in front of everyone early, while it is still cheap to change.

The reference photos and concepts on this page are Landforms work and Landforms properties. Nothing here is a stock library, and no client project is shown without permission.

Project stories

We would rather publish your yard than a stock photo.

There are no customer quotes on this page because the product has not shipped, and inventing them would tell you nothing. Instead, this is an open invitation: if you have a before and after worth looking at — whether Landforms built it or you did it yourself — send it in and we will consider featuring it here with your name on it.

  • What is useful

    A before photo taken from roughly the same spot as the after, a note about what changed, and what you would tell someone starting the same project. Drone shots are welcome but a phone photo from an upstairs window works.

  • What happens next

    Every submission is read by a person. Nothing gets published without your written permission, and we will show you the layout before it goes live. You can withdraw a story at any time by replying to the same email thread.

  • What we will not do

    We will not edit your words into a marketing quote, use your address, or pass your details to anyone else. If a story is used, credit is however you ask for it — full name, first name, or “a homeowner, name withheld.”

Questions worth asking early

Straight answers, including the unflattering ones.

Is this a design service or software?

Software, with a design company behind it. You supply the photo and the direction; the tool produces the concept and keeps it editable. It works on a property anywhere in the world. If you want someone to build what you end up with, nothing about the tool requires you to hire us — use the concept to brief whoever you like.

How accurate is the concept?

It is accurate about position, scale and relationship — where a patio sits, how much lawn is left, whether a court fits with clearance. It is not a construction document. Material thickness, base depth, drainage detail and structural design still have to be engineered by a human, and quantities shown in the editor are sample data while that logic is being built.

What do you do with my photo?

It is used to generate your concept and nothing else. Your property is never published as an example unless you specifically ask us to feature it and confirm it in writing. There is no advertising network on this page and no analytics script running today.

I am a contractor. Does this replace my designer?

No, and it is not trying to. It replaces the part of your week that gets spent talking a client out of something they cannot picture, or redrawing after a walk-through. You keep control of the plan; the tool just makes the conversation visual enough that fewer decisions get reopened at install.

How do credits work?

Every plan includes a set number of credits rather than unlimited use. A credit is spent when the tool runs an AI generation or a revision, and the exact credit cost of an action is shown before you confirm it, so nothing is ever charged without you seeing the number first. Starter includes 20 credits once, the Homeowner Project Pass includes 150 credits once, Professional includes 400 credits each month, and the add-on Credit Pack adds another 100 credits at any time. Credit metering is still being built — nothing on this page is metered today, and we are not promising rollover of unused monthly credits until the billing side is finished.

What is actually working right now?

The interactive preview is open and free until launch, and the site you are reading is live. Concept generation, the editing model, pricing logic and the account system are still in development, which is why pricing is labelled early access and why the contact form drafts a real email straight to the team.

Which categories can it handle?

Patios and outdoor living, pools and spas, courts through Backyard Sports Center™, fencing and privacy screening, retaining walls, landscape lighting, and planting and lawn design. They are not separate products — they all happen inside one concept of one property. There is more on each of these in the PlotVisionAI blog.
