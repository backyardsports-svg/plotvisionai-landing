For landscape professionals

Sell the design once, instead of redrawing it after the walk-through.

PlotVisionAI™ is built by a design-build contractor for the part of the week that gets eaten by clients who cannot picture the plan. Concept from a site photo, notes pinned where they belong, one link the client can actually approve.

Darin Designs Built by a landscape contractor with 42+ years on job sites, not a software studio. Read the founder story

Where it pays

Fewer revision rounds, cleaner approvals.

Present in the driveway

Work from the site photo you already took. Show the concept on a phone before you leave, while the client is still standing in the yard you are describing.

Quantities travel with the picture

Materials, square footage and plant counts stay attached to the concept, so a change to the layout moves the takeoff instead of orphaning it in a spreadsheet.

Your name on the concept

Shared concepts carry your logo, not ours. The tool is meant to make your proposal easier to approve, not to sit between you and your client.

Straight answer on scope: this does not replace a designer, an engineer or a permit set. It replaces the part of the job where a client reopens a decision because they never really saw it. Read how it works for what a concept can and cannot decide.

Fit into an existing process

Concept first, then your real numbers.

  • Site visit. Photograph from the client's eye level and from the angle they complain about.
  • Concept pass. Generate a direction, then a second version so there is a real choice on the table.
  • Client session. Pin notes to coordinates. "Move this" beats "the left one, but not that part".
  • Takeoff. Carry quantities into your own estimating workflow — you price the work, not the tool.
  • Approval. Send one link. The concept, the notes and the scope in one place.
