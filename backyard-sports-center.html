Design type — Backyard Sports Center™

Backyard Sports Center™: see the court on your own yard before anyone pours a slab.

A backyard court is a big, permanent, expensive rectangle, and it is the single hardest landscape item to picture from a drawing. PlotVisionAI™ renders the court into a photograph of your own property, at the orientation, colour and containment you are considering.

Before and concept

See your court before you build

Same slab, same white vinyl fence, same trees, same camera position. The only thing that changes across the transition is the surface — which is exactly the decision a homeowner is trying to make when they are looking at a quote for a court.

Every concept frame on this page is labelled. Site photographs come from the property owner or from Landforms Design Inc; anything generated is marked as an AI concept visualization, and no frame is presented as documentation of a build history.

Why a court is the hardest thing to sell on paper

Homeowners can read a patio from a plan because they have stood on patios. A 30-by-50 slab in the middle of a lawn is different: is it too close to the deck, does it kill the sight line from the kitchen window, will it dominate the yard, where does the hoop pole land, how tall is the fence really, and what happens to the whole thing after eight in the evening. Those questions do not get answered by a dimensioned drawing. They get answered by a picture of your own yard with the court in it.

The pairs below work backwards from real finished courts: the court is removed and the bare yard is reconstructed, keeping the same camera position, the same house and roofline, the same trees and property boundaries, the same grade and the same daylight. Reading a pair left to right is the homeowner’s experience in reverse — the empty yard they are standing in, then the thing they are trying to imagine.

What you are buying

A concept visualization of your property, not a construction package.

The transition below is the deliverable in its plainest form: your site photo, and a concept generated from that same frame, at full resolution and without a watermark on a paid plan. PlotVisionAI is used by homeowners and landscape professionals worldwide, and it works on any property you can photograph — anywhere in the world.

The transformation runs automatically. What you buy is the right-hand frame: a concept visualization of your own property, generated from the photograph you upload, that you can hand to a builder, show a spouse, or attach to a bid comparison. What you are not buying is construction, engineering or a quote — base depth, aggregate, drainage, frost detail, pole footings, netting structure and local code all stay with the contractor who builds it.

What a court concept should decide before anything is ordered

Footprint and orientation — a 30-by-50 multi-sport pad reads very differently rotated ten degrees, and rotation is free on screen. Surface colour — red and grey against brick and mature hardwoods, or blue and green against evergreens. Containment — how high the fencing or netting has to be before it starts to feel like a cage from the kitchen window. Play after dark — whether the hour after a game finishes is usable at all, which is a night lighting decision rather than a court one.

What a concept does not decide: base depth, aggregate spec, drainage, frost heave, pole footings, tile versus post-tensioned slab, or the local code that governs pole height and light spill. A picture cannot carry those, and PlotVisionAI is explicitly not sold as construction documentation. The concept exists to get everyone agreeing on the same yard before a builder prices the real thing.
