Credits

How credits work.

  • Targeted concepts use 1 credit. Mark the area to change and preserve the rest of the property.
  • Specialist-category redesigns use 2 credits. Authorize a full redesign within the selected project type.
  • Full Backyard redesigns use 3 credits. Use this only when the whole yard should change.
  • You see the cost first. The credit cost of an action is shown before you confirm it, so nothing is charged without the number in front of you.
  • Free includes 5 starting credits. Enough to begin the workflow on one property, with watermarked exports.
  • Project Pass includes 150 credits on one property, once, at $89.
  • Professional includes 400 credits a month at $139, spendable across properties and client concepts.
  • Corporate plans start at $299 a month. Team, Enterprise and pilot options are available for firms.
  • Individual plans do not currently promise rollover. Contracted corporate team plans use separate written allowance and rollover terms.

Questions about which plan fits? Get in touch before buying, or read the FAQ. Current terms are on the terms page.
