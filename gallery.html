Matched pairs, real builds, and concepts labelled as concepts.

23 transformation samples across the eight design types. Every pair holds the property still and changes only the thing being decided — same house, same camera position, same light. Filter by design type, or show only the before-and-after pairs.

How to read the labels

Three labels, used literally

  • Before. The existing condition, photographed on site — by us or by the client.
  • Concept Visualization. AI generated from that photograph. Always badged. Never a finished build.
  • Completed Result. A photograph of work that was actually built. Never labelled AI.

Pairs transition automatically between the two frames; motion stops if your device asks for reduced motion. No client names, addresses, budgets or ratings are attached to any image on this page.
