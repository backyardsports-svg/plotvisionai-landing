Corporate and teams

Seats for the whole firm, credits in one pool.

Team plans put your designers, estimators and sales staff on one workspace drawing from a single credit allowance, so nobody runs out mid-bid while someone else’s balance sits unused. Prices below are the approved corporate rates.

Team plans

Four team sizes, then Enterprise.

Every team plan is the same product; the difference is how many seats it covers and how much the pooled allowance is. Annual billing is twelve months for the price of ten.

Team plans include assisted activation. Choose Team 5 through Team 25, or request an Enterprise plan, and the pre-filled request gives our team everything needed to complete setup. Nothing is charged and no seats are created until we reply and confirm the details with you. The 30-Day Pilot can be purchased immediately.

Corporate terms

What you are agreeing to.

  • Annual is twelve months for the price of ten. Team 5 is $2,990 a year, Team 10 is $4,990, Team 15 is $6,990 and Team 25 is $9,990.
  • Credits are pooled. One allowance for the account, spent by whichever seat needs it, rather than a separate balance per person.
  • Corporate team-plan credits roll over for 90 days, capped at twice your monthly allowance. Anything above that cap, or older than 90 days, expires.
  • Credit packs are $249 for 1,000 credits and $999 for 5,000. They top up the shared pool and do not change the plan.
  • Onboarding is $750, waived on annual plans. It covers workspace setup and getting your team started.
  • The 30-Day Pilot is $299 one-time for up to 10 seats and 500 credits. That payment is applied to an annual contract signed within 14 days.
  • Plans renew automatically at the end of each billing period until you cancel.
  • There are no refunds for unused or partial periods. Cancelling stops the next renewal; it does not refund time or credits you did not use.
  • Enterprise adds $32 per additional seat per month above the contracted base, from 26 seats up.

These corporate terms sit alongside the general terms of use. Consumer plans are on the pricing page, and the FAQ covers how credits are spent.
